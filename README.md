# Cine Capture Pro

Free, open-source software for digitising 8mm, Super 8 and 16mm cine film
with a modified projector, a hall-effect frame trigger and a USB camera.

**Status:** in development (phase 1, capture core). Not ready for use yet.

## What it does

1. **Capture** – one frame per projector trigger, saved exactly as the camera
   delivers it, with locked camera settings and dropped-frame detection.
2. **Edit** – trim and join reels, stabilise (perforation and/or film edge,
   horizontal and vertical), crop, colour-correct and restore. Non-destructive.
3. **Export** – ProRes, H.264/H.265, DNx, FFV1, DPX and image sequences, with
   optical sound.

## Platforms

Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). A macOS version is planned.
